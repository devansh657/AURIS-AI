# AURIS Inbound Phone Assistant

## Implemented Contract

AURIS 0.8.4 contains a provider-neutral call policy and a Twilio ConversationRelay adapter. A signed incoming-call webhook returns TwiML that connects the call to the signed WSS relay. Caller turns are handled in ephemeral memory. Urgent, protected-data, commitment, or explicit human requests end the relay with handoff data; the signed action callback then dials only Devansh's configured E.164 number.

The opening greeting identifies AURIS as Devansh's AI assistant, states that provider transcription is used, says AURIS does not record audio, and asks callers not to provide passwords, security codes, or payment details.

Durable call records contain only:

- Provider call ID and masked caller suffix.
- Importance and handoff result.
- Factual summary, caller request, urgency, and suggested follow-up.
- Confirmation that disclosure occurred and raw audio/transcript were not persisted.

## Required Deployment Configuration

The cloud service requires these secret-managed environment values:

```text
AURIS_TELEPHONY_ENABLED=true
AURIS_TELEPHONY_PUBLIC_URL=https://phone.example.com
AURIS_TELEPHONY_RELAY_URL=wss://phone.example.com/v1/telephony/relay
AURIS_TELEPHONY_OWNER_NUMBER=+44...
AURIS_TWILIO_AUTH_TOKEN=<secret-vault reference>
AURIS_TELEPHONY_LANGUAGE=en-GB
AURIS_TELEPHONY_TTS_PROVIDER=ElevenLabs
AURIS_TELEPHONY_VOICE=<approved provider voice, optional>
```

Configure the purchased voice number's incoming webhook as `POST /v1/telephony/incoming` and status callback as `POST /v1/telephony/status`. The ConversationRelay URL and `Connect` action are generated by AURIS. Every HTTPS and WSS request is rejected unless its `X-Twilio-Signature` validates against the exact configured public URL.

The provider's current contract requires a voice-enabled number, ConversationRelay onboarding, a `wss://` application endpoint, and signed webhook validation. See the official [incoming voice webhook](https://www.twilio.com/docs/usage/webhooks/voice-webhooks), [ConversationRelay](https://www.twilio.com/docs/voice/twiml/connect/conversationrelay), [WebSocket message](https://www.twilio.com/docs/voice/conversationrelay/websocket-messages), [Dial](https://www.twilio.com/docs/voice/twiml/dial), and [webhook security](https://www.twilio.com/docs/usage/webhooks/webhooks-security) references.

## Production Gates

Do not enable live calls until all of the following are evidenced:

- Provider account, compliance profile, purchased number, and AI/ML onboarding.
- Trusted public TLS and WSS ingress, secret vault, rate limiting, abuse monitoring, and production identity.
- Jurisdiction-specific review of AI disclosure, transcription consent, privacy, retention, emergency language, and call transfer.
- Devansh's transfer number and transfer-hours policy.
- Real acoustic tests for latency, interruption, transcription quality, voice naturalness, failed transfer, disconnect recovery, and emergency handling.
- A secure notification path for new summaries on the laptop and enrolled mobile device.

Until those gates pass, the local portal reports telephony as `CONFIGURATION REQUIRED` or `DEPLOYMENT REQUIRED`, and no real call is claimed.
